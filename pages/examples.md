# AVAnnotate Examples
Explore projects created by users to see how the platform supports a range of research, educational, institutional, and individual goals on the [AVAnnotate website](https://av-annotate.org/example-projects/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. 

For a curated list of possible approaches and annotated example projects, see the [Approaches and Annotated Example Projects](https://avannotate.github.io/guidelines/pages/approachesexamples.html#researchers){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} in our [User Guidelines and Resources](https://avannotate.github.io/guidelines/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

Finally, [the AVAnnotate Monster Mash](https://avannotate.github.io/mm/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} serves as documentation of the breadth of AVAnnotate features.
