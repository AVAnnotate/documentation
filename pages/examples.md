# AVAnnotate Examples
Explore example projects created by users on the [AVAnnotate website](https://av-annotate.org/example-projects/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} to see the range of projects the platform supports. 

For a curated selection of possible approaches and annotated example projects organized by audience, see the [Approaches and Annotated Example Projects](https://avannotate.github.io/guidelines/pages/approachesexamples.html#researchers){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} in our [User Guidelines and Resources](https://avannotate.github.io/guidelines/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.
